From Echoes to 3D Scenes

Progress in acoustic imaging and reconstruction

Ruixu Geng · The University of Hong Kong

Hi all, for this group reading, I will share some recent work on acoustic imaging and 3D reconstruction, in air and under water. The papers are listed below.

Synthetic apertures, arrays and imaging around corners

  • An in-air synthetic aperture sonar dataset of target scattering in environments of varying complexity (Scientific Data, 2024)doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-04050-0
  • Fusing Concurrent Orthogonal Wide-aperture Sonar Images for Dense Underwater 3D Reconstruction (IROS, 2020)arxiv.org/abs/2007.10407
  • Seeing With Sound: Long-Range Acoustic Beamforming for Multimodal Scene Understanding (CVPR, 2023)doi.org/10.1109/cvpr52729.2023.00101
  • Acoustic Non-Line-Of-Sight Imaging (CVPR, 2019)doi.org/10.1109/cvpr.2019.00694
  • Ultrasound synthetic aperture non-line-of-sight imaging (Communications Physics, 2025)doi.org/10.1038/s42005-025-02335-3

Reconstruction from sonar data

  • Neural Volumetric Reconstruction for Coherent Synthetic Aperture Sonar (ACM TOG, 2023)arxiv.org/abs/2306.09909
  • SH-SAS: An Implicit Neural Representation for Complex Spherical-Harmonic Scattering Fields for 3D Synthetic Aperture Sonar (3DV, 2026)arxiv.org/abs/2509.11087
  • Neural Implicit Surface Reconstruction using Imaging Sonar (ICRA, 2023)arxiv.org/abs/2209.08221
  • A Theory of Fermat Paths for 3D Imaging Sonar Reconstruction (IROS, 2020)doi.org/10.1109/iros45743.2020.9341613
  • Bathymetric Surveying With Imaging Sonar Using Neural Volume Rendering (IEEE RA-L, 2024)arxiv.org/abs/2404.14819
  • SonarSplat: Novel View Synthesis of Imaging Sonar via Gaussian Splatting (IEEE RA-L, 2025)arxiv.org/abs/2504.00159
  • Acoustic Neural 3D Reconstruction Under Pose Drift (IROS, 2025)arxiv.org/abs/2503.08930

Sonar together with a camera

  • Opti-Acoustic Stereo Imaging: On System Calibration and 3-D Target Reconstruction (IEEE TIP, 2009)doi.org/10.1109/tip.2009.2013081
  • AONeuS: A Neural Rendering Framework for Acoustic-Optical Sensor Fusion (SIGGRAPH, 2024)arxiv.org/abs/2402.03309
  • Z-Splat: Z-Axis Gaussian Splatting for Camera-Sonar Fusion (IEEE TPAMI, 2025)arxiv.org/abs/2404.04687
  • Opti-Acoustic Scene Reconstruction in Highly Turbid Underwater Environments (IROS, 2025)arxiv.org/abs/2508.03408
  • Sonar-MASt3R: Real-Time Opti-Acoustic Fusion in Turbid, Unstructured Environments (ICRA, 2026)arxiv.org/abs/2603.13585
  • VisualEchoes: Spatial Image Representation Learning Through Echolocation (ECCV, 2020)arxiv.org/abs/2005.01616
  • Beyond Image to Depth: Improving Depth Prediction using Echoes (CVPR, 2021)arxiv.org/abs/2103.08468

Coded sensing and acoustic holography

  • Compressive 3D ultrasound imaging using a single sensor (Science Advances, 2017)doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.1701423
  • Single-sensor multispeaker listening with acoustic metamaterials (PNAS, 2015)doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1502276112
  • Holograms for acoustics (Nature, 2016)doi.org/10.1038/nature19755
  • Holographic acoustic elements for manipulation of levitated objects (Nature Communications, 2015)doi.org/10.1038/ncomms9661
  • A volumetric display for visual, tactile and audio presentation using acoustic trapping (Nature, 2019)doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1739-5
  • GS-PAT: High-speed multi-point sound-fields for phased arrays of transducers (ACM TOG, 2020)doi.org/10.1145/3386569.3392492
  • Differentiable Geometric Acoustic Path Tracing using Time-Resolved Path Replay Backpropagation (ACM TOG, 2025)doi.org/10.1145/3730900
  • Temporal acoustic point holography (SIGGRAPH, 2024)doi.org/10.1145/3641519.3657443

I will mainly discuss what a single echo can and cannot measure, and how these works recover the 3D position that an echo alone leaves out.

For background on synthetic aperture sonar: Synthetic Aperture Sonar: A Review of Current Status (IEEE J. Oceanic Eng., 2009) and Introduction to Synthetic Aperture Sonar (InTech, 2011).

Ruixu Geng, WeLight Lab, The University of Hong Kong
